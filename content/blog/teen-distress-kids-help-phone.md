Teens often talk more easily to a counsellor than to a parent in the same kitchen. This guide clarifies when [Kids Help Phone](https://kidshelpphone.ca) is a good next step, and when you need emergency services instead.

Kids Help Phone serves young people in Canada (generally under 29) with professional counselling — call [1-800-668-6868](tel:18006686868) or text CONNECT to 686868, or use the options on kidshelpphone.ca. You can sit nearby while they reach out, or give them privacy if that is safer.

If there is immediate danger — a suicide attempt in progress, severe injury, violence — call [9-1-1](tel:911). For suicidal crisis where 9-8-8 is the right national line, call or text [9-8-8](tel:988) as well. Kids Help Phone is support; it is not an ambulance.

As a caregiver, you can also use adult pathways: [ConnexOntario](https://connexontario.ca) for Ontario service navigation, [988.ca](https://988.ca) for crisis, and our [Resources](/resources) page for verified directories. Do not invent local teen clinic phones.

Ask one open question after they connect: “Do you want me to help with a next step, or just know you’re not alone tonight?” Respect the answer unless safety requires you to act.
