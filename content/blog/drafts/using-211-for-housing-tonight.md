When housing falls apart tonight, you need a live intake path — not a scraped list of shelter phones. This guide explains how to use 211 quickly and what to have ready.

Dial **211** or visit [211.ca](https://211.ca) in Canada, [211ontario.ca](https://211ontario.ca) in Ontario, or [211.org](https://www.211.org) in the United States. Tell the specialist you need emergency shelter or housing help tonight. Share city, number of people, ages if relevant, and any accessibility or safety needs.

In Toronto, Shelter Central Intake is also listed on our [Resources](/resources) page: [416-338-4766](tel:4163384766) or [1-877-338-4766](tel:18773384766), or dial 311 in Toronto. We do not publish individual shelter numbers because vacancies change constantly. Our guide on [finding Toronto shelter help](/blog/finding-toronto-shelter-help) covers that local path in more detail.

If someone is in immediate physical danger, call [9-1-1](tel:911). If suicidal crisis is also present, call or text [9-8-8](tel:988). Housing lines and crisis lines can both be needed in the same night.

Write down the referral you receive, the callback number, and where to go next. Keep one bag of essentials ready if a placement becomes available.

If you want to keep going privately, you can [talk with Liam's Call AI](https://liamscall.com/) — free, no account required.
