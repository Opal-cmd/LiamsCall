Searching “detox near me” often returns ads and closed clinics. This guide helps you prepare a [ConnexOntario](https://connexontario.ca) call so the referral is based on what is open now.

Call [1-866-531-2600](tel:18665312600) or use the contact options on connexontario.ca. Say you are looking for withdrawal management or addiction treatment for yourself or a loved one in Ontario. Have ready: city or region, approximate age, substances of concern if known, and whether housing is also unstable.

Ask what is available today versus waitlists. Ask whether medical detox is needed. You do not need a perfect clinical history. Do not invent clinic phone numbers from old blog posts.

If someone is overdosing or in immediate danger, call [9-1-1](tel:911) first. For suicidal crisis, call or text [9-8-8](tel:988). ConnexOntario is for service navigation, not emergency dispatch.

For a broader overview of this search pattern, see our earlier guide on [Ontario detox near me](/blog/ontario-detox-near-me). In the U.S., use the [SAMHSA National Helpline](https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline) instead of ConnexOntario.
