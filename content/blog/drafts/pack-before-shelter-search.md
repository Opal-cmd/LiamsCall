A shelter search moves faster when one small bag is already ready. This guide lists practical essentials families can gather without turning the moment into a full move.

Use [211](https://211ontario.ca) (or [211.ca](https://211.ca) / [211.org](https://www.211.org)) for live housing and shelter navigation rather than inventing facility phones. While you wait on a referral, pack light: government ID if available, medications in original bottles if possible, a phone charger, a change of clothes, hygiene items, and any documents related to income or benefits.

Skip valuables you cannot replace. Label a bag with a first name and a contact number for the supporting family member. If children are involved, pack one comfort item and ask 211 about family-appropriate placements.

Confirm a meeting point if you get separated. Note any accessibility needs before the intake call. If mental health or substance use is also active, say so when you reach 211 — systems often need more than one pathway.

If the situation involves violence or immediate danger, call [9-1-1](tel:911). For suicidal crisis, call or text [9-8-8](tel:988). Packing does not replace those lines.
