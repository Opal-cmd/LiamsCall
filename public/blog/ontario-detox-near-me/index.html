Ontario Detox Near Me: How to Find Help That Is Actually Open

Searching Ontario detox near me? ConnexOntario matches people to current withdrawal management and addiction treatment — safer than outdated Google clinic lists.

Published

July 15, 2026

← All stories

“Ontario detox near me” is one of the most common searches people make when substance use has tipped from worrying into urgent. Caregivers type it for a partner or adult child. People in distress type it for themselves. What Google often shows is a mix of ads, old directories, and clinics that closed or changed their intake months ago.

You do not need a perfect map of every detox bed in the province. You need a live referral.

In Ontario, call ConnexOntario at 1-866-531-2600 or visit connexontario.ca. They help people find mental health, addiction, and crisis services — including withdrawal management (detox) and treatment programs — based on what is available now. Phone, chat, text, and email options are listed on their site.

For health questions that are not an emergency but still feel pressing, Ontario’s Health 811 line connects you with a registered nurse. Dial 811. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. For suicidal crisis or severe distress, call or text 988.

A few practical notes while you wait on the line or chat: have the person’s age, city or region, and substances of concern if you know them. Ask whether they need medical detox, residential treatment, or day programs. If they are also housing-unstable, say so — the system often needs both pathways started, not one secret “best” clinic.

Liam’s Call is not a treatment centre and does not hold bed lists. We can help you prepare for the ConnexOntario call and keep you oriented while you wait. For the current verified numbers in one place, see our [resources page](/resources). When the search for “Ontario detox near me” gets loud, one verified intake line beats twenty guesswork tabs.

Quick steps

  1. Call ConnexOntario — Call 1-866-531-2600 or visit connexontario.ca for live addiction and detox referrals.
  2. Share practical details on the call — Note age, city or region, substances of concern, and whether housing is also unstable.
  3. Use Health 811 for non-emergency health questions — Dial 811 to speak with a registered nurse in Ontario.
  4. Escalate in a true emergency — Call 911 for immediate danger, or call or text 988 for crisis support.
